About Us

Your trusted partner for construction material logistics across Bangladesh

Delivering Quality Materials Since Day One

Altaf Trade and Transport specializes in the safe and timely delivery of construction materials — rod (MS steel), cement, sand, and bricks — to project sites across all 64 districts of Bangladesh. Whether you're building a home, a commercial complex, or an infrastructure project, we ensure your materials arrive on schedule and in perfect condition.

What We Transport

Four essential construction materials — delivered anywhere in Bangladesh

🔩

MS Rod / Rebar

All grades & sizes of reinforcing steel bars for structural work. Transported securely on flatbed trucks.

🧱

Cement

Bulk and bagged cement from all major brands. Covered transport to protect from weather.

🏖️

Sand

Sylhet sand, local sand, and coarse sand. Measured in CFT with accurate loading.

🧱

Bricks

First-class, second-class & picket bricks. Careful loading to minimize breakage.
